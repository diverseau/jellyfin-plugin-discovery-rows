Enable shelves on your homepage

  1. Open Home Screen Sections in the plugin dashboard. Choose Discovery Rows shelves, set their order, and save HSS settings.
  2. Open Modular Home in the menu and enable shelves for your user.
  3. Refresh the homepage. Shelves below the configured minimum stay hidden.

Start with 6–10 discovery shelves alongside Continue Watching and Next Up. Distinct order numbers keep shelves stable.
